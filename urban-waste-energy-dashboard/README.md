# Urban Waste & Energy Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## System Architecture

Operational business intelligence dashboard for monitoring, analyzing, and visualizing municipal waste flows and circular energy recovery.

## Overview

This project delivers an interactive reporting suite for tracking material balances and energy yields across urban treatment facilities. The architecture integrates heterogeneous public datasets to provide operational and strategic insights for circular economy planning.

### Workflow
1. **Data Integration:** Harmonizes disparate datasets covering incoming waste tonnage, automated sorting recovery, biomethanation yields, and thermal generation.
2. **Analytical Modeling:** Time-series normalization across monthly, quarterly, and multi-year horizons categorized by waste stream fraction.
3. **Geospatial & Facility Drill-Down:** Visual mapping of regional treatment infrastructure with multi-level filtering (district, facility, fraction, temporal range).
4. **Stakeholder Dashboards:** Role-specific views balancing day-to-day operational throughput against macro sustainability KPIs.

## Operational Impact

- **Context:** Waste management and byproduct energy recovery generate fragmented data silos across physical plants and administrative layers.
- **Engineered Solution:** Unified data model aggregating mass-balance metrics, material diverted from landfill, and net energy output (biogas and electrical power).
- **Application:** Resource allocation, contract compliance monitoring, and environmental impact reporting.

## Technical Stack

- **Business Intelligence & Modeling:** Power BI (DAX, Power Query)
- **Geospatial Mapping:** ArcGIS visual integration
- **Data Layers:** Municipal public data registries, material flow accounting schemas
